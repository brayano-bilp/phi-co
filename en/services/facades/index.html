Our services

Façades

Curtain walls, façade renovation and composite-panel exterior cladding.

Façades are our core business. We design, manufacture and install glazed envelopes and exterior cladding for office buildings, bank headquarters, hotels and institutional buildings, both new and refurbished.

  • Saint-Gobain

  • Curtain wall
  • Façade renovation
  • Exterior cladding (Alucobond, Trespa, Fundermax)

Curtain wall

Lightweight glazed façades on aluminium framing (stick, semi-structural and structural glazing systems), with insulating and solar-control glass suited to the equatorial climate. Thermal studies and calculation notes supplied with every project.

Façade renovation

Restoration and modernisation of existing façades: cleaning, substrate repair, waterproofing treatment and new finishes, including rope access on high-rise buildings.

Exterior cladding (Alucobond, Trespa, Fundermax)

Aluminium composite and compact laminate cladding: ventilated rainscreens, cassettes, canopies and column casings. Wide choice of colours and finishes for a durable, low-maintenance façade.
