About us

A Cameroonian company built to international standards

PHI & CO S.A is a company incorporated under Cameroonian law with a share capital of 100,000,000 FCFA. Founded in 2005 and restructured in 2009 and 2019, its activities are the engineering, production and installation of building-finishing works, mainly façades, glazing and supplies.

PHI & CO S.A was born from its founders’ will to deliver, in Cameroon, works that meet international standards. Its name refers to the golden ratio φ, the “divine proportion”: harmony of proportions guides our approach to the building envelope.

  • 100 M FCFA share capital
  • 4 sites in Douala: factories, warehouse, showroom
  • 2 representative offices in Europe: Geneva and Cordignano

Our history

  1. 2005

    Foundation

    PHI & CO is founded in Douala with the ambition of bringing international-standard façades and glazing to Cameroon.

  2. 2009

    First restructuring

    Reorganisation of the company and scale-up of production capacity.

  3. 2019

    Public limited company

    New restructuring: PHI & CO becomes an S.A with a share capital of 100,000,000 FCFA and structures its network of European partners.

  4. Today

    Regional reference

    Four sites in Douala, two representative offices in Europe and projects for the country’s major institutions.

How we work

For each project we carry out a targeted feasibility study according to the client’s requirements. Our projects start with:

  1. The final proposal submitted to the client;
  2. On-site measurements and coordinates taken with precision instruments (laser scanner, total stations…);
  3. Shop drawings and execution details, including catalogues and technical specifications;
  4. Calculation notes and samples of the products to be supplied.

For works requiring specific expertise and highly technical operations, the company relies on its network of international technical partners.

Our commitments
