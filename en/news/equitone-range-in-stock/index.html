Discover EQUITONE, elegance in façades – now in stock

17 May 2024

EQUITONE fibre-cement façade panels are available immediately from PHI & CO.

At PHI & CO we are proud to present EQUITONE, our range of fibre-cement façade materials combining modern aesthetics and lasting performance. Designed to inspire and transform your spaces, EQUITONE panels give an elegant finish and a unique character to every architectural project.

Authentic texture and colour

Each EQUITONE panel has a natural texture and a refined colour palette, ensuring harmonious integration with your creative vision. Whether you are looking for a raw, industrial look or a touch of finesse, EQUITONE meets your aspirations.

Durability and resistance

Resistant to fire, water and UV, EQUITONE panels protect and enhance your buildings for years to come.

Immediate availability

We understand how important availability is to complete your projects: the EQUITONE range is now in stock at PHI & CO, ready for delivery.
