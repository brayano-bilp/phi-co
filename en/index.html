Our method

Every project starts with a rigorous study

  1. 01

    Proposal and feasibility study

    Analysis of your requirements, choice of systems and a final priced proposal.

  2. 02

    Precision surveys

    On-site measurements and coordinates with laser scanner and total station.

  3. 03

    Drawings and specifications

    Shop drawings and execution details, catalogues, technical specifications, calculation notes and samples.

  4. 04

    Manufacturing and installation

    Production in our Douala factories, then installation by our teams, backed by our international technical partners for highly technical works.

Why choose Phi & Co

  • Local production

    Two production centres in Douala (Yassa and Akwa): CNC cutting, assembly and insulating glass made on site, for controlled lead times.

  • International standards

    Certified systems from major European manufacturers, installed according to their technical specifications.

  • International network

    Representative offices in Geneva and Cordignano (Italy) and technical partners for complex works.

  • Field experience

    Almost twenty years of office, banking and hotel projects in Cameroon.
