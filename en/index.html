Discover the company

Our method

Every project starts with a rigorous study

  1. 01

    Tailored proposal

    A final proposal adjusted to each client’s unique specifications.

  2. 02

    Precise measurements

    State-of-the-art instruments – laser scanners and total stations – guarantee accurate project data.

  3. 03

    Drawings and specifications

    Detailed drawings, varied catalogues and technical specifications for a clear understanding of the project.

  4. 04

    Technical validation

    Calculation notes and product samples to ensure compliance and quality, then manufacturing and installation.

Why choose Phi & Co

  • Local production

    Two production centres in Douala (Yassa and Akwa): CNC cutting, assembly and insulating glass made on site, for controlled lead times.

  • International standards

    Certified systems from major European manufacturers, installed according to their technical specifications.

  • International network

    Representative offices in Geneva and Cordignano (Italy) and technical partners for complex works.

  • Field experience

    Almost twenty years of office, banking and hotel projects in Cameroon.
